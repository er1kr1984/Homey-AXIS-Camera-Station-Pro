Watch your AXIS Camera Station Pro cameras live in Homey, without connecting Homey to each camera.

The app gets live video from Camera Station's own WebRTC service, the same one its web client uses. Add your cameras and Camera Station views as devices, see them live on your dashboard, and use motion and offline alarms in your flows.

What you can do:
- Watch cameras live, in Low, Medium, High or Automatic quality (Low by default, gentle on Homey).
- Add a Camera Station split view as a device, shown as one picture laid out like in Camera Station.
- Use the "Camera Station live" dashboard widget to watch a whole view live.
- Get motion alarms and other events from Camera Station action rules.
- Start and stop manual recording, add bookmarks, move PTZ cameras to presets, and use Camera Station triggers from flows.
- Watch the health of your Camera Station server: disk problems, failed recordings and cameras that go offline.
- Take snapshots for push notifications.

What you need:
- AXIS Camera Station Pro with its web client (tested with version 6.16).
- A Camera Station user with rights for live view. For recording, bookmarks and PTZ, the user also needs those rights.
- Homey and Camera Station on the same local network. Live video goes directly between Camera Station, Homey and your phone; it is not sent through the cloud.

Away from home, the dashboard widget plays the video through Homey's normal remote connection, or shows pictures if that doesn't work.
